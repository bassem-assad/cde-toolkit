Seven tools, one sign-in

Everything you do over and over in ACC, in one place.

Each tool works on its own. Sign in once with your Autodesk account and move between them from the top bar.

Move, check and fix files

CDE Sync

Move files into ACC from ProjectWise, SharePoint, InEight Document, another ACC project or a local folder. Maps attributes, reads the destination folder's naming standard, flags values that don't fit, and adds droplist values only when you choose.

Autodesk sign-in

File validator

Check a file-list export from ACC Docs against your naming convention and confirm required attributes are filled in. Download the failures as a CSV report.

Excel or CSV export

Rename

Add the upload date of the current version to a Revit model's name, with a preview of the new name before anything changes.

Autodesk sign-in

Coordinate models

Federated views

Choose a location code and create a Model Coordination view of every published model with that code, without opening ACC. Folder and attribute names are set to match your project conventions.

Autodesk sign-in

GeoFed

Federate ACC models on a map. View 3D and 2D side by side, take snapshots with markup and check model coverage by location.

Autodesk sign-in

Control and report

File Book Out

Find drawings by attribute, see their booking status and book them out through an ACC review. Files already booked out are left out of the review and listed.

Autodesk sign-in

Review turnaround

See where reviews slow down by step, reviewer and company, with late and overdue rates, from a review export.

CSV or JSON export
How it works

One setup step for the ACC admin, then nothing to install.

  1. Admin adds the app

    Your ACC hub admin adds the CDE Toolkit Client ID under Hub Admin, Custom Integrations. It's a one-off step, and it's also your organisation's approval.

  2. People sign in as themselves

    Each person signs in with their own Autodesk account. The toolkit can only do what that person's ACC permissions already allow.

  3. Work in the browser

    Files go directly between the source system and ACC. Nothing is uploaded to or stored on our servers.

Requirements

Check these before you pilot it.

You need

  • An ACC (Autodesk Forma) hub on a full subscription such as Forma Data Management, Forma Build or Forma Design Collaboration.
  • A hub admin who can add a Custom Integration.
  • Model Coordination, for Federated views.
  • A current version of Chrome or Edge.

Optional sources for CDE Sync

  • ProjectWise and InEight servers must allow the toolkit's web address (CORS).
  • SharePoint needs a one-off Microsoft Entra app registration.
  • Local folders and CSV attribute exports need nothing extra.
Trial and Essentials hubs can't use itAutodesk Support notes that the Custom Integrations tab is hidden for trial hubs and for hubs on Essentials subscriptions. Those hubs can't add CDE Toolkit. Ask your admin whether the tab is visible before you plan a pilot.
Security and data

Built so your files don't pass through us.

  • The toolkit runs in your browser. Files travel between your source system and Autodesk, not through our servers.
  • Sign-in uses Autodesk's OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. There's no client secret and no password is stored.
  • The sign-in token stays in your browser tab and is cleared when you sign out or close the tab.
  • There are no advertising or analytics scripts on this site or in the app.
  • Tools that change data (CDE Sync, Rename, File Book Out, Federated views) show what will happen before they do it. Test on non-critical projects first and keep your own backups.
  • Read the Terms of Use and Privacy pages.
