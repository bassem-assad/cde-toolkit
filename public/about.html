About

I'm Bassem Assad.

I'm a BIM manager and digital engineering manager based in Australia. I trained as an architect and now specialise in digital engineering and information management.

My work is the part of a project that keeps its information usable: the common data environment, Autodesk Construction Cloud (Forma), Revit, and the naming, metadata and review conventions that hold them together.

CDE Toolkit brings together the tools I've built for those tasks, so other teams can move, check, book out and rename files, and build federated views, without doing it by hand.

What I care about

  • Your files stay with you.The toolkit runs in your browser and doesn't take a copy of your project data.
  • No surprises.Tools that change data show what will happen before they do it.
  • Your conventions come first.Several tools read your project's own naming standard and attributes, or let you set the names they look for.
