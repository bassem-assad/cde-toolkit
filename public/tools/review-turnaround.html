Review turnaround

See where reviews slow down: by step, reviewer and company.

Try the demo Request a pilot CSV or JSON export

Who it's for

  • BIM and information managers, project directors and delivery leads who need to show or improve review performance.
  • Consultants and contractors who want evidence of how quickly they respond.

Why it helps your company

  • Find the real bottleneckSee which step, reviewer or company is behind, instead of guessing.
  • Facts for the conversationLate rates by company make programme discussions easier to have.
  • Nothing to set upDrop in an export and read the answer.

What it does

  • Reads a CSV or JSON file with one row per review task, and recognises the columns: review, step, reviewer, company, assigned, completed and due.
  • Shows the number of tasks, the average turnaround, the share that were late or overdue, and the slowest step.
  • Breaks the results down by step, reviewer and company, with bars coloured by how late they run.
  • Counts open tasks as late once they pass their due date, but leaves them out of the averages.
  • Reads dates such as 24/10/2026 as day/month.
Example scenario

A project director suspects reviews are slow. In the demo's sample data, Final approval averages 3.5 days with 42% of tasks late, while author checks average 1.2 days. The conversation moves from opinions to the step that needs attention.

What you need

  • A CSV or JSON file with one row per review task. At minimum it needs a reviewer column and an assigned date column.
  • No sign-in. The file stays in your browser.

See the requirements for hub plans, and the admin guide for the one-off setup.

Good to know

  • It reports on the file you give it. It doesn't keep history between sessions.
