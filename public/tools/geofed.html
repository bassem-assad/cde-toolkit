GeoFed

See your federated models on a map, check what's missing, and share snapshots.

Try the demo Request a pilot Autodesk sign-in

Who it's for

  • BIM managers who want to see model coverage by location and discipline.
  • Project directors and asset owners who think in places, not folders.
  • Coordinators checking that models are viewable and placed correctly before a milestone.

Why it helps your company

  • Gaps visible earlySee which locations lack a discipline before the coordination meeting, not during it.
  • Fix the metadata that blocks federationQA lists the files with missing codes or models that won't open.
  • A common picturePlace and discipline are easier for non-BIM stakeholders to read than folder trees.
  • A shareable listExport the QA report to the people who need to fix things.

What it does

  • Browse your hub, project and folders, tick the folders to federate, and choose which file types count as models or drawings.
  • Groups models by location and discipline and shows them on a map with open basemaps (streets, aerial, topographic and more).
  • Shows 3D and 2D views beside the map, with Map, Split and 3D layouts, plus Properties, Drawings and Issues panels.
  • Coverage matrix: what exists for each location and discipline, with gaps hatched. Click a cell to filter.
  • QA checks: missing location code, missing discipline code, models not viewable, loaded models without map coordinates, georeference ignored, and latest version rejected in review. Export the QA report as CSV.
  • Saved views, and snapshots with markup.
Example scenario

Before a coordination milestone, the BIM manager opens Coverage and sees that one location has no civil or landscape model. The QA list shows four files with no discipline code. Both lists go to the discipline leads the same day.

What you need

  • An Autodesk sign-in and models stored in ACC.
  • Location and discipline attributes on your files (you choose which attributes to use).
  • Models are translated by Autodesk's viewing service so they can be displayed.
  • Internet access to the map tile providers.

See the requirements for hub plans, and the admin guide for the one-off setup.

Good to know

  • The demo shows the layout and the Coverage and QA panels with sample data. The 3D viewer needs a real sign-in.
  • Models are placed using their georeference where available, or the locations you set.
